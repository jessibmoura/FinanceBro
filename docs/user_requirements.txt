1. Data Ingestion Requirements (Input Channels)
IN-01 — CSV Invoice/Statement Parsing:
Support file uploads for bank statements and credit card invoices.
Extract dates, descriptions, amounts, and installment metadata ($N/M$).
Enforce deduplication via SHA-256 fingerprint matching before saving to the database.
IN-02 — Natural Language Entry (Pix / Real-Time Spending):
Process plain-text inputs (e.g., "Gastei 45 no almoço via Nubank hoje" or "Pix de 120 pro condomínio").
Automatically extract: Amount, Category/Subcategory, Payment Account, and Date (defaulting to current date/time).
Respond with confirmation and prompt for quick clarifications if any required variable is missing.
IN-03 — Single-Registration Recurring Expenses:
Allow users to register a recurring obligation once (name, estimated amount, due day, target account, and category).
Persist and project these recurring records indefinitely across subsequent calendar months without requiring re-entry.
2. Data Management & User Stories (Configuration & Organization)
US-01 — Budget Caps per Category: As a user, I want to set monetary spending caps for each root category so that I can prevent overspending during the active month.
US-02 — Short & Long-Term Financial Goals: As a user, I want to create goals with target amounts, target dates, and priority classifications (short-term vs. long-term).
US-03 — Bank Account & Credit Card Profiles:As a user, I want to register bank accounts, debit accounts, and credit cards with metadata including institution name, statement closing day, and payment due day. Link every transaction directly to its originating account.
US-04 — Savings Balance by Account:As a user, I want to track reserved funds (emergency reserves, investments, dedicated savings) segregated by their respective bank institution.
3. Analytical & Visibility Requirements (Dashboard Visual Outputs)
VIS-01 — Month-to-Date (MTD) Expenditure: Display the aggregated real-time total of all settled expenses from day 1 of the active cycle up to the current day.
VIS-02 — Projected Month-End Total:Calculate and display the expected month-end liability:$$\text{Projected Total} = \text{MTD Expenses} + \text{Remaining Unsettled Recurring Bills} + \text{Contracted Installments}$$
VIS-03 — Budget Tracking by Category:Display total spending per root category juxtaposed against its predefined cap (numerical delta and percentage progress bar with threshold color alerts).
VIS-04 — Granular Subcategory Breakdown:Provide secondary drill-down tables and charts breaking down expenditures at the child subcategory level.
VIS-05 — Net Cash Flow (Liquid Balance):Present a comparative KPI:$$\text{Net Balance} = \text{Actual Inflows (Income)} - \text{Actual Outflows (Expenses)}$$
VIS-06 — Financial Goals Progress:Show percentage fulfillment gauges ($\frac{\text{Current Allocated}}{\text{Target Amount}} \times 100$) for both short-term and long-term milestones.
VIS-07 — Maturing Installment Cash Flow Relief Notice:Detect transactions marked with final installments ($N = M$). Display an informational alert highlighting upcoming cash flow liberation:"Your final installment for [Description] clears this month. R$ [Value] will be freed up in your budget starting next month."
